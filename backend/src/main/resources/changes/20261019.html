Drawers with slots: see at a glance how much room is left

A location at the end of the tree is often a drawer divided into compartments, one kind of component per compartment. You can now say so: Locations → Edit has a new Number of slots field. Leave it empty for a location that is not divided.

Every component type with stock in the drawer takes one slot, and once the last piece of a part is taken out, its slot counts as free again. The Locations tree shows each drawer's state in a badge such as 3 of 12 slots free, turning red when the drawer is full or holds more kinds of parts than it has slots. Wherever you choose a location to put parts away (New Part, Quick Add, adding or moving stock on a part, the barcode scanner and kit generation) the free slots are shown next to the drawer's name. A full drawer never stops you storing something, it is only shown as full.

A drawer with slots holds parts, not further locations, so it cannot be given sub-locations, and a location that already has sub-locations cannot be given slots. An AI assistant connected through AI access (MCP) sees the slot counts as well, so it can suggest where a new part will still fit.
