Organisations

Sortiment can now hold several completely independent inventories side by side. An organisation owns its own parts, stock, locations, categories, spec fields and tags — nothing is shared, and nothing from one organisation is visible from another. Two organisations can even catalogue the same part number without colliding.

All your existing data has been moved into a single organisation called Initial Organisation. Nothing has been lost or renamed, and if you only ever use one organisation the app behaves exactly as it did before. You can rename it under Organisations.

The organisation you are working in is shown at the bottom of the sidebar, just above your name. If you belong to more than one, click it to switch — the app reloads with that organisation's data. The same switcher is on My Account.

Managing organisations

A new Global Administrator permission grants access to the Organisations screen, where organisations are added, renamed and removed. It has been given to everyone who could already manage users, so nothing needs setting up.

There is also a Template organisation, holding a copy of the categories, spec fields and tags as they were. Every new organisation starts as a copy of it, so a fresh organisation begins with a full, usable taxonomy instead of an empty screen — and no parts, locations or stock. Edit the Template to change what new organisations start with. Only Global Administrators can see or switch into it.

Users are assigned to one or more organisations on the Users screen. Every user needs at least one.

Locations are now shared within an organisation

Locations used to belong to individual users, and you could only add or take stock in your own. They now belong to the organisation, so everyone in it sees the same locations and can work with the stock in any of them. The owner column and the per-user restriction on adding, taking and moving stock are gone.

Who did what is still recorded: every stock movement keeps the name of the person who made it, and each part still shows who added it. On the dashboard, the old By User table has been replaced by By Location, which breaks the organisation's stock down per top-level location instead.

Because locations are no longer personal, new users are no longer given a starter location — pick the organisations they belong to instead, and create locations on the Locations screen.
