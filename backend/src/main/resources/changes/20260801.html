Organisations

Sortiment can now hold several completely independent inventories side by side. An organisation owns its own parts, stock, locations, categories, spec fields and tags — nothing is shared, and nothing from one organisation is visible from another. Two organisations can even catalogue the same part number without colliding.

All your existing data has been moved into a single organisation called Initial Organisation. Nothing has been lost or renamed, and if you only ever use one organisation the app behaves exactly as it did before. You can rename it under Organisations.

The organisation you are working in is shown at the bottom of the sidebar, just above your name. If you belong to more than one, click it to switch — the app reloads with that organisation's data. The same switcher is on My Account.

Permissions are now per organisation

A permission used to be a property of your account, and applied everywhere. That is the wrong shape once there is more than one organisation: being allowed to edit parts in one says nothing about another. From now on every permission except Global Administrator is held per organisation — the same person can be an administrator in one organisation, an ordinary member in the second, and absent from the third.

Nothing changed for anyone in practice: existing permissions were copied into every organisation their holder belongs to, so everyone can do exactly what they could before.

There is a new Organisation Admin permission. It grants the organisation-level administration tasks — the Admin Actions screen — and control over who belongs to the organisation and what they may do in it. An Organisation Admin cannot see or change what someone is allowed to do in an organisation they don't administer.

The old Manage users permission is gone. Seeing the organisation's members, adding people to it and changing what they may do are all part of administering an organisation, so they now follow Organisation Admin and nothing else. Everyone who held the old permission was made an Organisation Admin, so nobody lost anything.

Your permissions follow you when you switch organisation, so a screen or button may appear or disappear as you move between them. Changes to what someone may do take effect immediately, on their very next action — they do not have to log out and back in.

The Users screen

Users now lists the members of the organisation you are in, and the permission checkboxes set what they may do there. It is visible to Organisation Admins only. Use Add existing user to bring someone else's account into the organisation, and Remove to take them out again — their account itself is untouched, as is their membership of other organisations.

User accounts stay global: an email address identifies one person across the whole installation. Creating, editing and deleting accounts therefore requires Global Administrator. Inviting people by email will come in a later change.

All Users

The Users screen shows one organisation at a time, which is right for running an organisation but no use for running the installation. All Users — a new screen for Global Administrators — lists every account and the organisations each belongs to.

Select someone to open their full record: edit their name, email, phone and password, set their Global Administrator permission, and manage each organisation they belong to. You can add them to an organisation, remove them from one, and tick exactly what they may do in each — all from one place, without switching organisation yourself. Membership and permission changes are saved as you click them; account details have a Save button.

Two things the screen will not let you do, both to avoid a mess that cannot be cleaned up afterwards: removing someone's last organisation (delete the account instead), and removing your own Global Administrator permission — ask another Global Administrator, or there may be nobody left who can give it back. Note that removing someone from an organisation also drops the permissions they had there, so re-adding them starts from nothing.

Managing organisations

A new Global Administrator permission grants access to the Organisations screen, where organisations are added, renamed and removed. It has been given to everyone who could already manage users, so nothing needs setting up.

There is also a Template organisation, holding a copy of the categories, spec fields and tags as they were. Every new organisation starts as a copy of it, so a fresh organisation begins with a full, usable taxonomy instead of an empty screen — and no parts, locations or stock. Edit the Template to change what new organisations start with. Only Global Administrators can see or switch into it.

Users are assigned to one or more organisations on the Users screen. Every user needs at least one.

Locations are now shared within an organisation

Locations used to belong to individual users, and you could only add or take stock in your own. They now belong to the organisation, so everyone in it sees the same locations and can work with the stock in any of them. The owner column and the per-user restriction on adding, taking and moving stock are gone.

Who did what is still recorded: every stock movement keeps the name of the person who made it, and each part still shows who added it. On the dashboard, the old By User table has been replaced by By Location, which breaks the organisation's stock down per top-level location instead.

Because locations are no longer personal, new users are no longer given a starter location — pick the organisations they belong to instead, and create locations on the Locations screen.
